I am a Ph.D. student at the [School of Software, Nanjing University](https://software.nju.edu.cn/). Before beginning my doctoral studies, I graduated from the [School of Computer Science at Nanjing University](https://cs.nju.edu.cn/).

My doctoral advisor is [Prof. Xintao Niu](https://niuxintao.github.io/). During my undergraduate studies, I was fortunate to receive guidance from the faculty members of the [Theory Group at Nanjing University](https://tcs.nju.edu.cn/).
